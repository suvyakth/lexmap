Lexmap · three 58-second videos

GSAP animations that render to MP4 in your own browser. Nothing is installed or uploaded.

1 · DemoDrives the real site: SF, LA, Dorchester, Hoboken + date, T1–T5, Spanish. 2 · TechHow it's built, three challenges and fixes, scores, remaining limitations. 3 · TeamWho you are, what you do, why the team is awesome. Edit the name first.

Make each video

  1. Open the video page in Edge or Chrome and press F11 (full screen gives the sharpest MP4).
  2. Click ▶ Preview once to watch it. Click Teleprompter to rehearse: a small window shows the line to say now and the next one.
  3. Tick Record my voice while rendering if you want to narrate in the same take, which needs no editing afterwards. Leave it unticked for a silent MP4.
  4. Click ● Render MP4. When the browser asks what to share, choose this tab, then allow the microphone if asked.
  5. After a 3-2-1 countdown (not recorded), the video plays for 58 s. The file lexmap-…-voice.mp4 or …-silent.mp4 lands in Downloads.

Tips

  1. Keep the teleprompter window small, and don't cover the whole video window with it.
  2. Captions are burned in by default (judges often watch muted). Untick them if you prefer.
  3. The demo needs internet for the map tiles. Run Preview once first so the tiles are cached.
  4. Upload each MP4 to Google Drive or YouTube (unlisted), and set the link to Anyone with the link.
