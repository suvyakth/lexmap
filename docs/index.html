Not legal advice. Lexmap shows what public housing law says, built from a fixed research corpus (retrieved Oct 1, 2026). It is not a compliance certification. Check the cited source, and talk to a lawyer or tenant organisation before acting.

Which housing rules apply at this address, and what is about to change?

Rent caps, just-cause eviction, deposits, fees, screening and algorithmic rent-setting across California, New Jersey and Massachusetts. Every answer quotes the law it comes from.

    Look up any address in CA, NJ or MA (live Census geocoding)

    The address is sent only to the U.S. Census Geocoder to find its legal city. Facts you leave blank are treated as unknown, never guessed.

    What's changing

    The five change cases from the challenge, computed by the same engine. Each one lists the addresses it affects and the before/after result.

    All extracted rules

    Every rule was extracted automatically from the corpus, verified against the source text, and reviewed by a second model pass. Click a row for its source quote and machine-readable coverage logic.

    Where the corpus has no rule

    Jurisdiction and category pairs for which no in-force rule was found in the supplied corpus. Lexmap reports these as gaps instead of guessing.

    How Lexmap works

    Rules compile to executable coverage logic, so each answer is an evaluation you can audit, not a generated guess.
